# CallDeskTech Cold Call Script

## Before You Dial
- Smile when you speak — they can hear it
- Stand or sit up straight — it changes your energy
- Have the caller’s name, business name, and any notes visible
- Goal: Book a 10-minute demo. Not to sell. Not to explain every feature.

---

## Opening (Gatekeeper)

> *"Hi, is [First Name] available?"*

**If asked who’s calling:**
> *"This is [Your Name] with CallDeskTech."*

**If asked what it’s regarding:**
> *"I’m calling about how [Business Name] handles incoming phone calls. It’ll just take a quick minute."*

**If they say he/she is busy:**
> *"Totally understand. When’s a better time to reach them? Right after lunch? Around 4?"*

- Get a specific time. Ask for their direct line or cell if possible.

---

## Opening (Decision Maker)

> *"Hey [First Name], this is [Your Name] with CallDeskTech. How’s your [day/morning/afternoon] going?"*

*(Pause. Let them answer. Don’t rush.)*

> *"I’m calling because we help [type of business, e.g., plumbing companies, restaurants, clinics] answer every call that comes in — even after hours — without hiring another person. Can I ask, do you guys ever miss calls during busy times or after 5?"*

**Why this works:**
- Low pressure. You’re asking about their pain, not pitching.
- Everyone has missed calls.

---

## If They Say YES (They Miss Calls)

> *"Yeah, that’s exactly what I figured. Most [business type] owners I talk to say the same thing. Here’s the quick version — we build an AI voice agent that answers your phone, books appointments, and answers common questions 24/7. It sounds natural, not robotic. [First Name], would it be worth a quick 10-minute demo next week to see if it makes sense for [Business Name]?"*

---

## If They Say NO (They Don’t Miss Calls)

> *"Got it — that’s actually rare, so you guys are doing something right. Out of curiosity, do you have someone answering phones full-time, or is that something you handle yourself?"*

*(Get them talking. Find the pain: cost of a receptionist, owner tied to the phone, hours limited.)*

> *"So if you had a way to answer calls at 8 PM or on weekends without paying overtime, would that be useful?"*

*(If yes → pivot to booking demo.)*

---

## The Close (Book the Demo)

> *"I’m not trying to sell you anything on this call. I just want to show you how it works in 10 minutes, and if it’s not a fit, no big deal. Does Tuesday or Wednesday work better for you?"*

*(Always give two options. Never ask “Are you free?” — it invites a no.)*

> *"Great. How’s Tuesday at 2 PM EST? I’ll send you a quick calendar invite with a Zoom link. What’s the best email for you?"*

---

## Objection Handling

### "Not interested" / "We’re good"
> *"Totally fair. Most people say that until they see how much cheaper it is than hiring someone. If it saved you $500–$1,000 a month on staffing, would it be worth 10 minutes just to look?"*

### "Send me an email"
> *"Happy to. Can I ask — do you actually want me to follow up, or is that the polite way of saying no? I’m okay either way, I just don’t want to bug you."*

*(If they really want an email, get it. If not, you saved time.)*

### "How much does it cost?"
> *"Great question. It depends on call volume, but most of our customers are in the $100–$400 a month range — way less than a part-time receptionist. I can get you an exact quote during the demo. Does Tuesday or Thursday work?"*

*(Never quote exact numbers on the cold call. It leads to price shopping without context.)*

### "Is this a robot?" / "I hate those AI things"
> *"I hear you — a lot of them are terrible. Ours actually sounds like a real person because we customize the voice and script for each business. That’s why the demo helps. You can hear it for yourself and decide."*

### "Call me back next month"
> *"Absolutely. Mind if I ask — is that better timing budget-wise, or are you just swamped right now?"*

*(This tells you if it’s a real objection or a brush-off.)*

- If real: *"I’ll put something in my calendar for [date]. Can I send you a one-pager in the meantime?"*
- If brush-off: *"Got it. I’ll check in next month. If anything changes before then, feel free to call me at [number]."*

### "We already have a receptionist / answering service"
> *"That’s awesome — a lot of our customers had one too before they switched. Usually they save 60–70% and their receptionist gets freed up for the stuff that actually needs a human. Want to see how the numbers compare in a quick demo?"*

## Voicemail (if you reach voicemail — keep it under 20 seconds)

> *"Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] with CallDeskTech. We help [business type] answer missed calls 24/7 with an AI voice agent. I’ll send you a quick email with a 90-second video. If it’s interesting, feel free to call or text me back at [number]. Thanks!"*

**Then:** Immediately send the email with a short Loom/video link.

## Tips
- Speak slightly slower than normal — nervous callers rush
- Use the person’s name 2–3 times during the call
- If they interrupt with a question, answer it in 10 seconds, then redirect back to booking
- Your only goal: get the demo. Don’t explain features. Don’t demo over the phone.
- If someone is rude, say *"No problem, have a great day"* and move on. Never argue.
